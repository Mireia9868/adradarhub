FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions that come up before someone runs their first scan.

Last updated · 2026-10-28

What exactly does AdRadarHub do?

You enter a domain and a market. It pulls what that advertiser and its closest competitors are running across ad transparency libraries, ranks the creatives by signal strength, and turns the result into a next-round creative brief.

Which platforms are covered?

Meta, Google, Bing, YouTube and TikTok for ad intelligence, plus Google Search Console and GA4 for your own site performance. Current live/demo status per platform is on the data sources page.

Is the data real or sample data?

Results are labelled. When a connector is credentialed you get live data from the platform; when it is not, the interface marks the output as demo. We never present generated numbers as measurements.

How does the free quota work?

Ten pulls per day per account, resetting at midnight UTC+8. A scan costs one pull no matter how many platforms you select. Image and text generation share the same pool.

Does it cost anything?

It is free while in beta, including the daily quota. There is no card required and no paid tier announced yet.

Can it see a competitor's spend or ROAS?

No, and no tool can. Those numbers belong to the advertiser's account. What you get is creative, timing, placement and landing page — enough to reconstruct the strategy, not the P&L.

What is the creative iteration module for?

It merges client-level rules (brand, required elements, banned claims, market and platform constraints) with your own campaign CSV, then writes a brief that separates what to keep, what to change, and what to test next.

Do I need an account?

An account unlocks the daily quota and keeps your scan history. Signing up only needs an email and a password of at least eight characters.

Where does my data go?

Account data is stored in Supabase Postgres. API keys for Google and the AI providers live server-side only and are never sent to the browser. Details are on the privacy page.
