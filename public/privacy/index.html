Privacy policy

A short, plain-language version of what happens to your data.

Last updated · 2026-10-28

What we collect

  • Account data: email address and a password hash (scrypt). We never store the plaintext password.
  • Usage data: how many pulls you have used in the current day, and the domains you scanned, so your history is available when you return.
  • Optional analytics: if site analytics is enabled, aggregate page views collected through Google Analytics 4.

We do not ask for a phone number, do not sell data, and do not run third-party ad retargeting on this site.

Third parties we call

To produce a result, a scan may call the platform APIs listed on the data sources page, plus an AI provider for text and image generation. Requests contain the domain, market and prompt you entered — they do not contain your email or password.

Credentials for those providers are held server-side as environment variables and are never exposed to the browser.

Retention and deletion

Account and usage records are kept while your account exists. To delete your account and all associated records, email hello@adradarhub.com from the address you registered with; we will remove the data within 30 days.

Cookies and local storage

We use a session cookie to keep you logged in, and browser local storage for your language preference. Analytics, when enabled, sets its own cookies. There are no advertising cookies.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@adradarhub.com.
